The Traveling Monk and the Stone Soup

2026-09-09 · Stories of the World · a true story · 3-minute read

A monk was traveling. He was hungry, but he had no food of his own. He carried only an empty pot. He came to a village where the people were not willing to share their food. The monk went to a nearby stream. He filled his pot with water and dropped a large stone into it. He set the pot over a fire.

A villager saw the monk and became curious. "What are you making?" the villager asked.

"I am making stone soup," the monk replied. "It tastes wonderful. I would love to share it with you, but it needs just a little something more to make the flavor perfect."

The villager thought about sharing the delicious soup. He didn't mind giving a few carrots. So, he added them to the pot.

Another villager walked by. He asked about the soup. The monk explained it was stone soup, but it wasn't quite ready yet. More and more villagers came by. Each one added something else. One brought potatoes, another onions. Someone added cabbage, and another brought peas. One person added celery, and another a few tomatoes. Then came sweetcorn. Someone even added meat, like chicken, pork, and beef. Others brought milk and butter. Finally, someone added salt and pepper.

The monk carefully removed the stone. It was not something you could eat. A warm, tasty, and filling soup was ready. The monk and all the villagers shared the delicious meal together. They had all helped to create something wonderful, even though they started with just a stone and a pot of water.
